Services Offered

Tutoring Services

One-on One Tutoring

Personalized sessions tailored to meet each learner’s unique goals and needs.

Small Group Sessions

An affordable option that promotes peer learning and social motivation—ideal for families or friends who want to learn together.

Online or In-Person Delivery

Flexible scheduling with engaging, interactive tools. (In-person availability depends on location.)

Summer Learning Programs

Support to combat summer learning loss, maintain academic skills, and help struggling students catch up.

Early Literacy Intervention

Targeted support for emerging readers to build foundational skills in phonics, fluency, and comprehension.

Assessment-Informed Planning

Optional math and/or literacy assessments to identify strengths, gaps, and guide instruction.

Tutoring for Adult Learners

Support for adult students upgrading skills, preparing for exams (e.g., GED), or improving workplace literacy and numeracy.

Consulting Services

IEP/IPP Meeting Support

Help parents understand and prepare for school meetings. I can attend as an advocate and assist in interpreting education plans.

Professional Development

Workshops for schools and educators on topics such as math and literacy instruction, inclusive classroom practices, and trauma-informed education.

Assessment Review

Support for parents in understanding psychoeducational assessments, report cards, and school reports—what they mean and how to use them.

Behavior Support Planning

Collaborative development of positive, strengths-based strategies for home and school to address challenging behaviors.

Parent Coaching

Guidance on how to support learning at home, navigate school systems, and advocate effectively for your child.

Resource Creation

Custom learning materials, personalized instructional plans, and home practice packets tailored to student needs.

Learning Profile Development

Synthesis of assessment data, teacher feedback, and family insight to create holistic, strengths-based learner profiles.
