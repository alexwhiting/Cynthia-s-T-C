About Me

Hello, my name is Cynthia! I am a passionate and caring educator with over 20 years of experience supporting learners across Canada. My teaching journey has taken me from urban classrooms in Nova Scotia to remote Northern communities in Manitoba and Ontario, where I’ve worked as a teacher, principal, resource specialist, and consultant.

I hold a Bachelor of Science, with a double major in Math and Biology, a Bachelor of Education with Math and Science as my Teachable subjects, and two Master’s degrees in education - one in Curriculum Studies (with a focus on supporting learners with diverse learning needs) and one currently in progress in School Counselling. I’m also certified in multiple provinces and trained in trauma-informed care, restorative practices, and inclusive education strategies.

Whether I’m teaching math, building literacy skills, or consulting on education planning, I bring a heart-first, student-centered approach to every interaction. I believe all learners can thrive with the right support, and I’m here to help make that happen.
