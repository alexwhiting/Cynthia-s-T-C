About Me

Welcome to Cynthia's Tutoring & Consulting! My name is Cynthia, and I am passionate about helping students achieve their academic goals.

With over 10 years of experience in tutoring and educational consulting, I specialize in personalized learning strategies that cater to each student's unique needs. I hold a Master's degree in Education from Stanford University and have helped hundreds of students improve their grades and test scores.

My approach focuses on building confidence alongside skills, creating a supportive environment where students feel empowered to ask questions and take intellectual risks.
